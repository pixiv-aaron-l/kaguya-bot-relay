# Privacy Policy

Kaguya Relay does not collect, sell or share personal data.

When a post is created in a subreddit where the app is installed, the app reads the post's public information (title, link, author username, image URL, text preview) and sends it to the Discord webhook configured by that subreddit's moderator. This is the only data that leaves Reddit, and it is already public on Reddit.

The app stores the IDs of recently relayed posts in Devvit Redis for seven days to prevent duplicates. It stores the webhook URL in the subreddit's app settings, visible to the subreddit's moderators.

The app does not track users, use cookies or run analytics. Questions can be sent to the developer's Reddit account listed on the app page.
